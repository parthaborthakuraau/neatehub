01 / Flagship
RKVY RAFTAAR Three stage-based sub-programs · Ministry of Agriculture
Our flagship - a central-government program with three stage-based sub-programs (Navyam, Isanya, Saranya). Capital from ₹2L to ₹25L depending on stage.
Rolling intake
 └ Sub-program 01 / Student
Navyam Student First
Curriculum-integrated entrepreneurship pathway. Sandbox grants, lab time.
₹2L · Annual
 └ Sub-program 02 / Idea
Isanya Idea-stage residential
8-week residential at AAU. Lab access, agronomist mentorship.
₹5L · Cohort
 └ Sub-program 03 / Growth
Saranya Early & growth-stage
Capital up to ₹25L, milestone-tranched. GTM support, follow-on networks.
₹25L · Open · Jun
 02 / National
AIC Atal Incubation Centre · NITI Aayog
National-level incubation. Pan-India mentor network. Separate funder from RKVY.
Quarterly intake
 03 / State
ASRLM Assam State Rural Livelihoods Mission
Rural enterprise program for FPOs, producer-led startups, and livelihoods innovation.
Continuous
 04 / Campus
Student First Campus entrepreneurship at NE universities
Pre-incubation program for students. Workshops, sandbox grants, founder office hours.
Annual · Aug
 05 / Deep-tech
TIC (IITG) - AISF Joint with IIT Guwahati
Robotics, sensing, machine learning for agri systems. Engineering-heavy founders only.

Our focus areas

Six sectors where we go deep.

We don't back everything. These are the agri-allied sectors where our labs, mentors, and producer networks give founders a real, durable edge.

Agri-Input

Bio-stimulants, seeds, soil health, and crop-protection innovation.

68 ventures

Post-Harvest

Cold-chain, storage, grading, and aggregation routing.

42 ventures

Livestock

Dairy, poultry, and small-ruminant productivity and aggregation.

31 ventures

Aquaculture

Fish, prawn, and ornamental systems with sensing and feed innovation.

22 ventures

Food Processing

Value-addition, packaging, and Northeast-origin food brands.

54 ventures

Rural Fintech

Credit, insurance, and digital infrastructure for producers.

18 ventures
