Design system · Session 02

The diagram language. Photos prove. Diagrams explain.

A small set of line-animated SVG diagrams carries everything explanatory on this site - incubation journey, eligibility branching, the capital ladder, the ecosystem map. They share a vocabulary so a founder reading the third diagram doesn't have to relearn the first.

Rule 01
Line-only, no fill.

Paths and outlines do the work. Fills are reserved for active states - usually copper, occasionally tea.

Rule 02
Textile grid as substrate.

Warp + weft + a soft 45° diagonal - the abstracted muga/eri geometry sits at 6% opacity, never decorative.

Rule 03
Draw in, don't fly in.

Strokes draw from origin to terminus, 1.4s ease-out, with 200ms staggers. Reduced-motion shows the final state.

Rule 04
Type does the heavy lifting.

Mono labels (10px, 0.1em tracking) for state, Display for node names. No icons inside diagrams.

Diagram 01 · Process

The incubation journey, stage by stage.

A four-stage path from idea to scale, with explicit off-ramps. Used on the homepage and the For Founders page. Founders enter at any stage - most enter at 02 or 03.

The dashed copper line is the founder's actual path (variable); the soft line is the conceptual full journey.

Diagram 02 · Decision tree

Eligibility, as a branching tree.

Two decision points narrow seven programs down to two-or-three recommendations. The wizard runs this internally - the diagram makes the logic legible.

Diamond = decision. Pill = outcome program. Solid copper = strong-fit branch from your answers.

Diagram 03 · Comparison

The capital ladder across programs.

Grant ceilings, plotted. Reads left-to-right as venture-stage ascends. The y-axis is capital; the x-axis is loose chronology of when founders typically engage.

Solid bar height = grant ceiling. Width = typical duration. Hollow ring above each bar = optional follow-on capacity.

Diagram 04 · Ecosystem

NEATeHUB sits inside an ecosystem.

The institutions, funders, partners, and producer networks that NEATeHUB plugs into. We are not the centre of the universe - we are a node that connects research, capital, and producers.

Concentric rings: NEATeHUB at the centre, then funders + AAU (institutional layer), then mentors + KVKs + portfolio (operational layer), then producers (outer reach).
