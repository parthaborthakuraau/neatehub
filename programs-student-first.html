Overview

Pre-incubation. On campus. For students who haven't picked.

Student First is upstream of Navyam (which is inside RKVY). It exists for students who are exploring entrepreneurship as a serious option - not yet committed to one venture idea. We provide structured exposure, sandbox capital, and access to founders who built ventures while still in college.

Who it's for.

Undergraduate or postgraduate students at one of our partner universities. Any discipline - engineering, agriculture, business, social sciences. The only filter is interest, not credentials.

What it isn't.

Not a venture-building program - that's Navyam under RKVY. Not for non-students. Not online - we are deliberate that this happens in person, on campus.

What you get

Four pillars, semester-long.

01

Workshops

Weekly two-hour sessions on campus. Real topics, not theory.

  • Customer discovery
  • Validation methods
  • Unit economics
  • Pitch craft
02

Sandbox grant

Up to ₹1L per project for prototypes, pilots, or user research. No equity, no strings.

  • Apply mid-semester
  • Decision in 2 weeks
  • Single tranche
03

Founder office hours

Monthly small-group sessions with operating founders. Ask anything.

  • 4 founders / semester
  • NE-rooted operators
  • Small-group format
04

Pathway to Navyam

Strong Student First projects get fast-tracked into Navyam evaluation the following year.

  • Auto-shortlist
  • Skip first-pass review
  • 3-year track record
